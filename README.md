# Coffee-Sales-Dashboard#

## Overview
This script analyses coffee shop sales data from a dashboard visualization to identify trends and patterns in revenue generation. The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance, broken down by various categories such as total sales, orders, quantity, product categories, top selling items, weekdays vs. weekends, store locations and hourly sales. This information will provide insight on where to focus future business efforts.

## Problem Statement
The coffee shop is looking to optimize sales by understanding:

1.  **Overall Sales Performance:** Total revenue, orders, and quantity sold for the month of May 2023.
2.  **Sales Trends Over Time:** Day-by-day sales patterns and performance across specific time frames within May 2023.
3.  **Top Performing Products:** Identifying the most popular product categories and specific items driving revenue.
4.  **Weekend vs. Weekday Performance:** Comparing sales performance between weekdays and weekends.
5.  **Store Location Performance:** Determining which locations are driving the most revenue.
6.  **Sales by Day and Hour:**  Identifying peak sales hours and days of the week.

The goal is to utilize these insights to inform future sales strategies, inventory management, marketing efforts, and staffing decisions.

## Analysis from the Dashboard:

Here's a breakdown of key findings from the dashboard data:

**Overall Performance:**

*   **Total Sales:** $157K, a 31.8% increase ($37.8K) compared to last month (LM).
*   **Total Orders:** 33,527, a 32.3% increase (8.2K) compared to last month (LM).
*   **Total Quantity Sold:** 48,233, a 32.3% increase (11.8K) compared to last month (LM).
*   **Average daily sales:** $5,056

**Sales Trends Over Time:**
*   The sales are consistent over the course of the month. The lowest sales recorded being $4.2K, and the highest being $5.7K
*   There is a sharp decline in sales at the end of the month

**Product Performance:**

*   **Top Product Category:** Coffee ($60.36K) is the highest performing product category by sales.
*   **Top 3 Products by Sales:**
    1.  Barista Espresso ($20.42K).
    2.  Brewed Chai Tea ($17.43K).
    3.  Hot Chocolate ($16.32K).
*   **Strong Growth:**  Tea and Bakery are also high performing product categories.

**Weekend vs. Weekday Performance:**

*   **Weekday Sales:** $117K (74.41% of total sales)
*   **Weekend Sales:** $40K (25.59% of total sales)
*   Weekdays are driving the majority of sales.

**Store Location Performance:**

*   **Top Performing Location:** Hell's Kitchen ($52.60K).
*  All locations had relatively similar revenue between $51.70K and $52.60K.
*   All three locations had ~ 30% growth compared to last month.

**Sales by Day and Hour:**
*   **Peak Days:** Monday - Wednesday have the highest sales of the week at $25K each day.
*    **Lowest Day:** Sunday has the lowest sales at $19K
*   **Peak Hours:** 8 AM to 10 AM are peak times for sales.

## Conclusion
The May 2023 sales dashboard shows a strong overall performance across all metrics. Some actionable items the coffee shop should consider include:

1.  **Capitalize on Coffee and Tea:** Continue to promote and maintain these best-selling categories.
2.  **Weekday Focus:** Increase marketing towards those who purchase on weekdays, rather than weekends.
3.  **Inventory Optimization:** Ensure sufficient stock for best-selling items, especially during peak sales hours and days.
4.  **Location Strategy:** Determine why the Hell's Kitchen location leads in sales. The other stores may be missing out on key features that Hell's Kitchen is utilizing.
5.  **Adjust Staffing:** Adjust staffing to accommodate peak times of 8am-10am.
6.  **Evaluate End of Month:** Determine what caused the sharp decline in sales at the end of the month to improve future sales.

By using this data, the coffee shop can make informed decisions that will improve business processes, customer satisfaction and sales growth.
